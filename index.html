FAQ

Common questions

Is cryolipolysis surgery?

No. It is a non-surgical treatment that uses controlled cooling on a targeted area. There are no cuts.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel cold and pressure at first, then numbness. You can usually return to normal activity after a session.

How soon will I see results?

It varies. Fat reduction from cryolipolysis shows gradually over several weeks. Skin glow after HydraFacial is often visible straight away.

Who should not have these treatments?

Tell us if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or have sensitive skin. We will advise at your consult whether a treatment is suitable.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking is best so we can save your slot. Walk-ins are welcome when we have space.

How do I pay?

Ask us at booking. We will confirm accepted payment methods and package terms.
